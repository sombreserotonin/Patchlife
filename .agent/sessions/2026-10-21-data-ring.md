# 2026-10-21 Ring camera and doorbell records

Added 46 Ring records (category `camera` or `doorbell`) from Ring's "software security updates" support article, all `verified` by `npm run verify:evidence` in `Crawl` on the first run. 

Sources: the article exists in three ring.com versions, all reachable: UK (`/gb/en/`, footer "English (GB)"), Australia and New Zealand (`/au/en/`, "English (ANZ)", recorded as `AU`) and US (no prefix, "English (US)"). No host was blocked.

Method: one record per row of the UK camera and doorbell tables. AU or US evidence and region were added only when that page has a row for the same model with the same date. Same model means the same name after dropping a leading "Ring ", a trailing colour list or "(discontinued)", and treating "Gen" and "Generation" as equal; plus three US rows whose own text names the UK model ("formerly Video Doorbell Pro", "formerly Video Doorbell Pro 2", "also called Door View Cam"). Four US-only products with no UK counterpart got US-only records: Stick Up Cam (2nd Gen) Battery and Wired, Car Cam, Elite 4K.

Every record: `fixed_date`, `at_least: true` (table header "at least until"), and `conditions` for the four-years-from-purchase rule for devices bought new on ring.com or from Amazon as the seller. Every date on the pages is a full day, month and year, so nothing was skipped for precision.

Not added (need a human or a name mapping): US rows whose names differ from the UK names with no Ring-stated link (Battery Doorbell, Battery Doorbell Plus, Battery Doorbell Pro, Outdoor Cam Plug-In/Battery/Solar, Stick Up Cam Pro variants, Spotlight Cam (1st Gen) variants, Spotlight Cam Plus and Pro variants, Floodlight Cam Plus, Floodlight Cam Pro), and the AU row "Ring Video Doorbell" (no generation). Regional conflicts: Video Doorbell 4 is 2027 in UK and AU but 2029 in US; Spotlight Cam 4K Pro is 2031 in UK but 2030 in AU and US. Those records carry only the regions that agree with the UK date.
